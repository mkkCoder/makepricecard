Questions

Printable price list FAQ

Do I need an account to make a printable price list?

No. There is nowhere to sign up — there is no server to sign up to.

Where is my menu or price list stored?

In this browser, on this device. Clearing site data clears it. Export JSON or CSV if you want a backup.

Can I export a free PDF price list or menu?

Yes. Free tier includes the Minimalist template plus PDF and PNG export with a small footer credit. Pro removes the watermark.

What does Pro change?

Watermark off, four extra templates, and logo upload. Core editing and export stay free. Pro is a one-time $10 lifetime unlock.

Is client-side licensing “secure”?

Determined users can bypass browser checks. That is an intentional tradeoff for running without a backend. See also our Terms and Privacy pages.
