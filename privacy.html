Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Who we are

FastPriceCard is a client-side web app. For questions, see Contact.

What we collect

  • In the app: Business info, menu items, template choices, and optional logo data you enter stay in your browser storage (localStorage) on this device. We do not operate an application server that receives that content.
  • When you buy: Payment and license fulfillment are handled by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). Their privacy policy applies to checkout and billing data. License keys may be checked against Lemon Squeezy’s license validate API from your browser.
  • License unlock: A verified license key may be stored locally in your browser so Pro stays unlocked on this device.
  • Analytics: None. We do not run third-party analytics cookies on this site.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account to use the editor.
  • We do not upload your price list content to our servers (there is no app backend).
  • We do not sell your menu or business content.

Exports

PDF, PNG, JSON, and CSV exports are generated on your device. Once downloaded, those files are under your control.

Data retention & deletion

Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes local content and may remove a locally stored Pro unlock. Keep your license key so you can activate again. The site’s hosting provider may process normal web request logs when you load pages; that is separate from your menu content.

See Contact for purchase support and product questions.
